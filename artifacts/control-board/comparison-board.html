Micro-control study

Control language for a continuous resume editor.

This board isolates the small visual controls only. The editor flow stays fixed: stacked sections, in-place expansion, reorderable roles, and live preview. The question here is how disclosure and reorder controls should look and feel in production.

What to compare

Section disclosure, hover/focus signaling, drag affordances, and action reduction. The goal is not more UI, just a more mature control language.
