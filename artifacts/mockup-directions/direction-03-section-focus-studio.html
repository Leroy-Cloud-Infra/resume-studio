Section health

Hierarchy strategy

Document structure leads, controls follow.

The page should feel like one coherent artifact, not a stack of cards.

Guidance integration

Inline, section-aware, and tightly scoped.

Advice appears where the user is editing so context switching stays low.

Section drawer

Current section

Experience role 1

Fields update in a docked drawer instead of a full-width sidebar.

Why this works

It breaks the visual repetition of standard editor systems.

Users see the document first, then adjust the selected section in context.

Editing model

Section by section, with progressive disclosure.

Only the active section expands. Everything else becomes a concise summary.

Calm trust cues

Soft color, strong spacing, restrained motion.

The system feels editorial rather than app-store generic.

A bottom drawer or docked section inspector would let this concept scale without turning into a monotonous left-rail form stack.

Preview and editor stay linked, but the editor never becomes the hero.
