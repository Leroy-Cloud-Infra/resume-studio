Resume Studio concept study

Three ways to make the workspace feel calm, smart, and not like a CRUD form.

This index collects the three directions side by side so the layout philosophy is easy to compare before judging the visual polish.

What each direction optimizes

  • Direction 1 keeps the document in charge and makes editing feel editorial.
  • Direction 2 leans into AI SaaS energy without turning into a noisy dashboard.
  • Direction 3 breaks sidebar repetition by using a focused inspector and bottom drawer.

Direction 01

Quiet Editorial Desk

  • Workspace philosophy Preview-first, paper-like, restrained.
  • Editing model Contextual section cards, not a giant form stack.
  • Guidance Inline notes and quiet status pills.

Direction 04

Editorial Workbench

  • Workspace philosophy Document-first with a docked contextual inspector.
  • Editing model Section-aware, but with fewer persistent surfaces.
  • Guidance Ambient notes stitched into the page hierarchy.

Direction 02

AI Command Center

  • Workspace philosophy Signals and suggestions are part of the interface.
  • Editing model Assistant-led, with live metrics and targeted fixes.
  • Guidance Visible but not chatty, more like a control surface.
Open direction

Direction 05

Margin Notes Studio

  • Workspace philosophy More manuscript than interface, more margin than sidebar.
  • Editing model Section focus appears as overlays, not permanent panels.
  • Guidance Embedded as notes and annotations around the page.
Open direction

Direction 03

Section Focus Studio

  • Workspace philosophy Document first, inspector second, drawer third.
  • Editing model Section-by-section flow with a docked control strip.
  • Guidance Integrated into the section state, not a separate AI box.
